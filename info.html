Shipping

Lagos: 1 to 3 working days. Nationwide: 3 to 7 working days, depending on your location.

Every order is confirmed and arranged directly over WhatsApp, including delivery cost, so you always know the full amount before anything is dispatched.

Exact delivery fees by state and courier partner to be added here once finalised.

Returns & exchange

Because most pieces are printed and finished in small batches, we handle returns and exchanges case by case over WhatsApp.

If something arrives faulty or the wrong size was sent in error, message us within a few days of delivery with photos of the piece and we will sort it out.

A formal, published returns window and exchange policy will replace this section once set by the brand.

Care instructions

  • Sweatshirts: machine wash cold, inside out. Do not tumble dry. Do not iron directly on the print.
  • Caps: spot clean only, do not machine wash.
  • Shorts: machine wash cold, do not bleach.

Full care details are also listed on each product page.
